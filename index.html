RECYCLE RESPONSIBLY

Recycling Guide

Learn how to handle common recyclable materials.

🧴

Plastic

Check the plastic type and local recycling rules. Empty containers before disposal.

📄

Paper

Keep recyclable paper clean and dry. Avoid mixing it with food-contaminated waste.

🍾

Glass

Check whether your local service accepts the glass item. Handle broken glass carefully.

🥫

Metal

Empty accepted metal packaging and follow local collection instructions.

📦

Cardboard

Flatten clean, dry cardboard boxes before placing them in the appropriate collection.
